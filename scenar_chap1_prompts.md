## C1S4 — Léon devant la machine

**Asset(s)** : `assets/Leon_devant_machine.jpg`, `assets/Leon_devant_machine.mp4`

**Prompt image (Leonardo Phoenix / Nano Banana) :**

```
2D cartoon illustration, magical high-tech inventor's workshop. Léon, elderly
friendly inventor with white beard, round glasses, flat cap on his head, bright
orange knitted sweater, large full-length brown leather apron covering his chest
down to his knees, stands on the LEFT side of the frame. He holds up 3 colorful
paper cards facing the camera, each clearly showing a simple child drawing: a
cat, a sun, a house, cards large and readable.

Next to him (center-left), a whimsical tall machine made of wood and brass,
glowing blue circuits, floating magical runes, a big open funnel on top, and a
round glowing screen on the front (empty for now).

Composition: Léon and the machine occupy only the LEFT 70% of the frame. The
RIGHT 30% of the frame is reserved to later insert a standing character, so it
must show: a clear, unobstructed wooden workshop floor in the foreground (so
the character's feet will rest on visible ground), and a decorated back wall
behind it — high wooden shelves with glowing colored bottles, floating magical
orbs in the air, holographic blueprints pinned on the wall, warm amber and
magical blue lighting. No workbench, no table, no chair, no furniture, no
object on the floor on the right side. No character, no person, no green
screen, no empty area.

Children's storybook style, cozy warm magical mood, high detail, soft lighting.
```

**Prompt animation (Google Veo) :**

```
2D cartoon illustration, animated children's storybook style. Cozy magical
high-tech inventor's workshop, warm amber and magical blue lighting, gently
floating dust motes and tiny sparkles in the air.

In a centered composition, Léon — an elderly friendly inventor with a
fluffy white beard, round glasses, flat cap, bright orange knitted sweater,
and a full-length brown leather apron — stands beside his whimsical wooden-
and-brass machine. The machine has a LARGE OPEN BRASS FUNNEL on top (very
visible, like an upside-down trumpet) and a round glowing screen on its
front (initially empty). Behind them, decorated shelves with glowing colored
bottles, floating magical orbs, and pinned holographic blueprints fill the
background, edge to edge.

KEY ACTION — IMPORTANT : Léon MUST physically insert each card INTO the
funnel on TOP of the machine, one by one. The card travels DOWN into the
funnel and DISAPPEARS inside the machine before the drawing appears on the
screen. He does not just wave the cards or hold them in front — he clearly
DROPS each one INTO the funnel opening.

Sequence, smooth and gentle :

1) Léon stands smiling, holding three colorful paper cards fanned out in
   his hand — a cat drawing, a sun drawing, a house drawing. Cards are
   large and readable.

2) Léon raises the CAT card high above his head, positions it directly
   over the open funnel on top of the machine, and SLIDES IT DOWN into the
   funnel. The card visibly enters the funnel and vanishes inside. The
   funnel pulses with blue light. Immediately, a small cat drawing appears
   on the round screen with a soft sparkle.

3) Léon raises the SUN card, positions it over the funnel, and DROPS IT
   INTO the funnel. The card slides in and disappears. A warm yellow flash
   travels down the machine. The sun drawing appears on the screen next
   to the cat.

4) Léon raises the HOUSE card, positions it over the funnel, and PUSHES
   IT INTO the funnel. The card disappears inside. Tiny sparkles puff up.
   The house drawing appears on the screen next to the others.

5) Léon nods proudly. The machine's screen pulses softly with blue-gold
   light, showing the cat, the sun, and the house side by side.

Each card insertion takes about 1.5 seconds and the hand motion is
DELIBERATE and CLEAR : reach up → align with the funnel opening → release
the card → card falls inside.

Static camera, subtle breathing motion only. No new characters appear.
No text overlays. No abrupt cuts. Children's storybook tone, cozy and magical.
```
